Five measurements that make any room look professionally designed. Save this.

One. Hang art with its centre one hundred and forty-five centimetres from the floor. It's called the fifty-seven inch rule.

Two. Keep your coffee table forty to fifty centimetres from the sofa. Close enough to reach your cup.

Three. Hang a pendant seventy-five to ninety centimetres above the dining table.

Four. Give every seat at the table sixty centimetres of width.

Five. Keep walkways at least ninety centimetres wide. That's the kitchen planners' minimum.
