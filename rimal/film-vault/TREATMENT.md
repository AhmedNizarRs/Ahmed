# Rimal — حزمة المهندس المعماري الشاملة (The Ultimate Architect Tools Vault)

**Idea (one sentence):** An architect's tools are scattered across dozens of searches; the camera whips through eight tools one by one, then zooms out to show they were all one sheet — with the price sitting in its empty centre.
**Promise (on screen by 3 s):** حزمة المهندس المعماري الشاملة — كل أدواتك بمكان واحد
**Hook (0–3 s, sound off):** «أكثر شي يضيّع وقت المعماري؟» at 150 px, with the bundle's name above it (50 px) · CTA: «اكتب «حزمة» بالتعليقات» at 50 px, standing from 2.3 s
**Terms (said and shown):** حزمة المهندس المعماري الشاملة
**Audience · channel · how it's watched:** Arabic-speaking architects and interior designers · Instagram Reels (@rimalinterior) · phone, muted autoplay first
**Spec:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, ≈55 s with estimated voice timings (re-timed automatically from the real take) · deliverables: mp4, poster, srt

## Benchmark
- *Powers of Ten* (Eames): learn the zoom-out as the meaning (scale reveals that separate things are one) · don't take its look or its narration style.
- An architect's drawing sheet (viewports, dimension lines, a title block, a CAD crosshair): learn its grammar for framing footage · don't take HUD clutter, fake coordinates or blueprint-blue.

## Structures considered
1. Problem montage → product box → feature list on cuts → price (the shape of the earlier "Rimal V2" edit).
2. **One sheet, a ring of cells, a zoom-extents reveal:** each tool is a cell of a 3×3 sheet; the camera walks the ring right-to-left and closes on the empty centre.
3. A 3D vault door that opens on each tool.
**Chosen:** 2, because "خلّي أدواتك كلها بمكان واحد" can be *shown* by one camera move instead of said. Rejected: 1 is the film that already exists; 3 is the genre's cliché and needs assets we don't have.

## Dials
Clock: voice (ElevenLabs take, cut into 15 lines; every beat is derived from line starts and word cues) · Continuity: journey (whip pans along one sheet), one scale-through at the hero moment, no hard cuts
Register: clean/premium with kinetic type · Text policy: type as hero (the on-screen lines carry the message muted)
Constant the eye holds: the gold CAD crosshair — it hunts through the searches, drafts every window, leads every move, clicks the price · Accent = gold `#c9974a / #e6bd76` means "what is in the vault" (counts, name, price, the drafting hand) · Type roles: Reem Kufi (Arabic display), Marcellus (numerals, Latin brand), JetBrains Mono (labels)
Palette and fonts come from the Rimal site (`rimal/assets`).

## Arc
Hook (0–3 s): the question and the bundle's name, then searches pile up faster and faster · Turn at «تخيّل لو كلها عندك»: the crosshair sweeps the pile into one point · Hero moment = payoff: zoom extents, the question in the empty cell, silence, **25$**
Arc quantity: cells of the sheet filled — 1 (name) → 8 (all tools) → 9 (price)
Ending answers the opening by reveal of scale: the scattered searches of the opening become one sheet; the only thing left to type is «حزمة».
Actions that must be seen: searches piling up and collapsing; each window being drafted; counters stopping on their values; the zoom-out; the word being typed and sent.
Missing inputs and how they were handled: no footage of the 3ds Max scripts or the AI prompts → line art labelled SCHEMATIC, prompt list labelled EXAMPLE PROMPTS · two Drive clips (Profile Builder) carry another creator's watermark (@arq.recursos) and are left out.

## Sound plan
Source: synthesized from the timeline (D hijaz: drone, frame-drum pulse, plucked strings) · Silence before the price · Loudest non-voice hit on the price · Voice: ElevenLabs, Iraqi Arabic ad voice ("مؤمل العباس"), script below.

## Script (VO)
1. أكثر شي يضيّع وقت المعماري، مو التصميم نفسه.
2. البحث عن الأدوات.
3. تخيّل لو كلها عندك من البداية.
4. هاي هي حزمة المهندس المعماري الشاملة.
5. إذا شغلك على ريفيت، مكتبة فاميليز بحجم ستة وعشرين جيجابايت.
6. لأوتوكاد، أكثر من ثلاثة آلاف بلوك ذكي، تتحكم بأبعادها خلال ثواني.
7. وفوقها، أكثر من ستة آلاف بلوك إضافي.
8. لسكتش أب، أكثر من ثلاثين إضافة احترافية،
9. ومن ضمنها مولّد المطابخ.
10. ولثري دي ماكس، سكربتات تولّد النوافذ والأبواب والمطابخ.
11. وحتى الذكاء الاصطناعي، أكثر من خمسة آلاف برومت جاهز.
12. خلّي أدواتك كلها بمكان واحد.
13. وكم رح تدفع مقابل كل هذا؟
14. خمسة وعشرين دولار فقط.
15. اكتب كلمة حزمة بالتعليقات، ونرسل لك الرابط.

## DECISIONS (autonomous mode)
- DECISION: Arabic, in the dialect of the earlier "Rimal V2" edit, because that edit and the site are Arabic-first; its sentences are reused where possible.
- DECISION: ≈50 s instead of V2's 93 s, because a Reel keeps more viewers when each tool gets one beat.
- DECISION: brand look from the Rimal site (desert night, sand, gold), not V2's silver, because the site is the brand's own system.
- DECISION: the "1080 smart SketchUp blocks" claim from V2 is dropped, because its only footage is the watermarked third-party clip.
- DECISION: price shown as 25$ (as V2 says it); the store lists 24.99.
- DECISION: no burned-in subtitles; the on-screen type carries each line and an .srt is delivered for Instagram's caption track.
