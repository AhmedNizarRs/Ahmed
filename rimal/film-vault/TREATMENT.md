# Rimal · The Sand Table (طاولة الرمل)

**Idea (one sentence):** A single brass ball ploughs one continuous groove through dark sand, drawing each tool in the vault one at a time, until it asks "why draw everything from scratch?" and the one line splits into hundreds and fills the whole table at once, so the viewer feels that the vault gives them everything in one download.
**Promise (on screen by 3 s):** «حقيبة الأدوات المعمارية الشاملة» (The Ultimate Architect Tools Vault)
**Hook (0–3 s, sound off):** hook at 96 px · CTA «رمال · الرابط في البايو» at 48 px from frame 0, returns at the end with the URL
**Terms (said and shown):** أوتوكاد · ريفيت · سكتش أب
**Audience · channel · how it's watched:** Arab architects, interior designers, students · Instagram Reels @rimalinterior · muted autoplay first, then sound
**Spec:** 1080×1920, 60 fps, 34.5 s = 2070 frames · deliverables: MP4 with VO and score, poster, SRT

## Benchmark
- *Sisyphus kinetic sand tables (a steel ball drawn by a magnet under sand)*: learn the single ball, the one unbroken line, the hypnotic calm · don't take the polar spirals or the erase patterns.
- *Karesansui (raked-gravel garden) photography at low sun*: learn raking light that turns a 1 cm groove into a strong shadow line · don't take the stones, the moss, the Japanese setting.

## Structures considered
1. **One line, then all lines** (sand table): one ball draws each tool in sequence, the turn questions it, the hero multiplies it.
2. Exploding vault: a box opens and the tools fly out as 3D objects.
3. Kinetic type list: each product name slams on the beat.
**Chosen:** 1, because the bundle's whole argument is "one download instead of hunting for each file", which is exactly one line vs. all lines, and sand is the brand (Rimal = sands). Rejected: 2 is the unboxing cliché; 3 is the generic kinetic-type reel and close to the last film's type-as-hero.

## Different from the DALIL door film (6 axes)
Structure: list-that-turns (was a journey to a blackout) · Opening: macro of untouched sand with a resting ball (was a door in blind light) · Signature shot: a groove ploughed in sand under raking light (was a 3D clip snap) · Camera: top-down follow over a plane, then a vertical pull-up (was an orbiting 3D macro) · Score: voice-led, sand hiss as the material, one sub-boom (was a 90 BPM music grid) · Ending: the brand pressed into the sand like a stamp (was a corridor of glowing handles). Also 60 fps (was 30) and a voice (was silent).

## Dials

Register: clean/premium, 60 fps, critically damped camera (ζ 1.0), springs ζ 0.8 for text; no particles, no bloom, no film grain (the sand grain is the surface itself)
Text policy: one headline zone, in sync with the VO, doubling as subtitles · Constant: the brass ball / the groove
Accent = brass gold #e6bd76 means "the tool": the ball and the fresh groove glint; at the end the accent moves to the price.
Type: Reem Kufi (display), IBM Plex Sans Arabic 600 (lines), Jost (Latin numbers)

## Arc
Hook (0–3 s): resting ball on smooth dark sand, the bundle name. · Turn at 21.96 s: "لماذا ترسم كل شيء من الصفر؟" while the camera rises and shows how little one ball has drawn. · Silence 24.8–25.55 s. · Hero = payoff at 25.55 s: the ball splits into hundreds of points and an Islamic star tessellation fills the whole table in 1.4 s (the native move: one line becomes everything).
Arc quantity: share of the table drawn = 0% → 2% → 5% → 8% → 11% → 14% → 14% (seen wide) → 100%.
Ending answers the opening by a reveal of scale: the opening is one ball on empty sand; the end is the full table, stamped «رمال».
Actions that must be seen: the ball ploughing each drawing; the ball splitting; the tessellation filling; the wordmark pressing into the sand.
