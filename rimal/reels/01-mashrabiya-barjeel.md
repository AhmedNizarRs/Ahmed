# Reel 01: «خمسون درجة… ولا مكيّف»
**Account:** @rimalinterior · **Format:** Instagram Reel, 9:16, about 35 seconds · **Language:** Arabic voiceover and Arabic on-screen text

## The idea
Before air conditioning, houses in the Gulf stayed livable at 50°C by design alone. The reel shows two secrets: the **barjeel** (wind tower) and the **mashrabiya**. It ends on today's version, Abu Dhabi's Al Bahr Towers. The call to action sends viewers to Rimal's free **مختبر الزخارف** (Pattern Lab) to design their own mashrabiya tile.

**Why it works:** the hook is a question every Gulf viewer has wondered about. The content is local heritage, it teaches something, and it leads straight to a free Rimal tool.

## Production notes
- **Voice:** a warm, calm male or female voice in Modern Standard Arabic with a light Gulf feel. Use a documentary tone, not an advertising one. Pause about 0.5s after «كيف؟».
- **Look:** Rimal's palette. Night brown `#120c09`, deep brown `#3b1f17`, sand `#e9dcc6`, gold `#c9974a`. Warm, grainy, golden-hour light.
- **Footage:** Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood (Al Bastakiya), Dubai, for the barjeel shots; your own mashrabiya shots, or patterns exported from the Pattern Lab; Al Bahr Towers (use licensed footage or a simple animation).
- **Sound:** soft oud or ambient desert pad. A low wind sound under the barjeel scene and a wooden "clack" on each number card.
- **Captions:** burn in Arabic subtitles, since most Reels are watched on mute.
- **Loop:** the last frame (a pattern on sand) matches the first frame's colours, so the video loops back to the hook.
