# Rimal Interiors — 30 days of video ideas (@rimalinterior)

One video per day, made one by one. Each idea has a hook (the first 2 seconds), a simple outline, a question for the comments, and a soft call to action.

**Who it's for:** architects, interior designers and students who use AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp and 3ds Max.
**What we sell:** digital tools on Gumroad, and one free Pattern Lab on the website.

## Rules for every video
- **Format:** 9:16 Reel, 15–35 seconds. The hook goes on screen as text in the first 2 seconds. Add captions in Arabic and English.
- **Facts:** only use numbers that appear on the site (see the product list at the bottom). Don't promise hours saved or results we haven't timed. If we show a time, we record a real timer on screen.
- **Free first:** at least every third video gives something away (Pattern Lab, free 3ds Max scripts). It earns trust before we sell.
- **The question:** pin it as the first comment and say it out loud in the video. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- **Arabic:** the Arabic hooks below are drafts. Have a native speaker check them before posting.
- **Draft, don't post:** nothing goes live until Ahmed approves it.
