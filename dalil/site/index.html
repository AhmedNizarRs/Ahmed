The problem

Independence ends at the door.

Floor, exit, room: all on walls, out of reach of a hand.

21.8 million

people in MENA (the Middle East and North Africa) live with moderate-to-severe vision impairment. [SOURCE]

A small clip that makes a handrail readable.

  1. Clip

    Snaps on in 2 seconds. No tools.

  2. Find

    A sliding hand meets it without searching.

  3. Read

    Braille and a raised number: Floor 2. Exit left.

    ⠋⠇⠕⠕⠗ ⠼⠃“Floor 2” in braille

No app. No battery. No renovation.
