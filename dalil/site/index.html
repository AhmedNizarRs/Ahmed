The problem

Independence ends at the door.

Floor, exit, room: all on walls, out of reach of a hand.

21.8M

people in MENA (the Middle East and North Africa) live with moderate-to-severe vision impairment.

The DALIL film

A 15-second film with music and no speech. On screen: The handrail is already in their hand. It says nothing. DALIL, a clip on the rail. A raised numeral and braille. A grammar a hand can read. Measured on site, printed to fit. When the lights fail, it still speaks.
