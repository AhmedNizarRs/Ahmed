Privacy note

This note explains what happens to the details you share through our form. Last updated 5 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number and organisation.
  • What you’re interested in, and, if you choose to share them, your investor type, typical ticket size and message.
  • How you reached the page, for example by scanning our QR code.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about DALIL: investing, running a pilot, mentoring or keeping you updated on our progress. We don’t use it for anything else.

Where it goes

Netlify, the company that hosts this website, handles the form. Netlify stores each submission in our account and emails it to the two DALIL founders. A copy may also be kept in a private Google Sheet that only the founders can open, and we may send you one thank-you email. Only the founders can see your details.

Who we share it with

No one. We don’t sell your details or share them with anyone else.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies and no advertising trackers. To learn which parts of the page are useful, we count visits anonymously with PostHog: the pages and sections viewed, buttons used, how you arrived (for example the QR code), your device type and rough location (country and city). We don’t record your screen, we never send what you type into the form, and nothing is stored on your device, so each visit is counted on its own. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, nothing is counted.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for our conversation with you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, or to stop hearing from us, email dalilwayfinding@gmail.com.
