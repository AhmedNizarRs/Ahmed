Audience: architects, interior designers, students arriving from Instagram, mostly on phones. Action: open a product's buy link. Proof: working Sharjah studio, public tutorials, real follower/view counts. Constraints: bilingual with live switch, no invented prices, reviews, or specs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The storefront is the studio's materials sample board: each digital product is a mounted specimen with a spec tag, not a SaaS pricing card or a luxury-interiors mood page.

OWN-WORLD: Deep walnut board ground, cream specimen tags with brown ink, sand-gold for pins, rules and the one action colour. Logo knot as the only ornament. Specimens: rectangular panels with a pinned tag, fine gold rule, soft lifted shadow. Type: classical serif caps for display (echoes the wordmark), a clean humanist sans for text, geometric Kufi for Arabic display.

STORY: Visitor sees the board, recognises the three tools they use, reads what is inside, trusts the studio, taps "Get it".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left (right in Arabic): logo tag, headline, one line of offer, gold primary "Browse the library" and quiet Instagram link. Right: the sample board with the three product specimens pinned at slight angles; tapping one lifts it and jumps to its entry. Language switch top corner.
