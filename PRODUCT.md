# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro, static output. The site is a storefront that sends buyers to existing product links, so it needs no server or database. Astro builds plain HTML with minimal JavaScript (fast on any connection), keeps the product list as data rather than hand-copied markup, and deploys to any static host. Deploy target: not decided.

## Users

Prospective customers of the business's digital products. They arrive wanting to understand what a product is, judge whether it is worth buying, and buy it.

## Product Purpose

A business landing site that presents the business's digital products and gets visitors to buy them. Success means a visitor finds the right product, trusts it, and completes a purchase through its product link.

## Positioning

Not yet stated. Record what makes these products different before writing persuasive copy; do not invent a claim.

## Operating Context

- Purchase happens through existing product links (external checkout or product pages). The site's job is to route qualified visitors to them, not to run its own checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: buy / order a product.
- Open decisions:
  - Business name (a brand name exists but has not been provided yet).
  - What the digital products are (type, count, prices).
  - Which platform hosts the product links / checkout.
  - Deploy target and domain.
  - Language(s) of the site.

## Brand Commitments

- An existing logo and brand name must be used. Files and name not yet provided; request them before any visual work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo / brand name: exists, not yet in the repo.
- Product links: exist, not yet provided.
- No testimonials, reviews, sales figures, or customer logos have been provided. Do not fabricate any of them.

## Product Principles

1. Every page moves a visitor toward a specific product's buy link.
2. Show the real product; claims must be ones the business can back up.
3. Fast and simple to purchase: minimal steps between interest and checkout.
