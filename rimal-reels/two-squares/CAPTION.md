## English
Every star in this wall is just two squares. ✦
Turn one 45°, repeat it on a grid, and the gaps become crosses. That's star-and-cross, tiled on walls in Iran in the 1260s.

Change one angle and you get a whole new pattern. Try it free in our Pattern Lab, link in bio, and export your tile as an SVG for a screen, a feature wall or a render.
