Questions

      

        

          What files can I send?
          
STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP files all work. No model yet? Send a sketch or a photo with measurements and we will design it.

        

        

          How is the price worked out?
          
By the material used, the time on the printer and any finishing. The studio estimate uses the same inputs, and the final quote is confirmed before printing.

        

        

          How long does an order take?
          
Most single prints take a few hours on the printer. Your quote includes a ready date so you know when to expect it.

        

        

          Can I get a different size or colour?
          
Yes. Every shop item comes in three sizes and any of our filament colours. For something outside that, use the custom studio.

        

        

          Do you ship?
          
Yes. Shipping is added to your quote based on where you are, so the total is clear before you pay.
