          Map from the wall plan

          No floor plan? Almost every building has one on the wall: the fire evacuation plan near the lifts and stairs. Take a photo of it and DALIL turns it into a navigation map.

          1. Stand straight in front of the plan and take the photo.
          2. Drag the 4 yellow corners to the plan's corners.
          3. DALIL finds the walls. Open any doorway it closed with your finger.
          4. Walk a few metres to set the size, then tap the rooms to name them.

          Map a building

          No floor plan? Walk the building with your phone and DALIL draws the map.

          1. Stand at the main entrance and tap Start.
          2. Walk along the middle of each corridor, phone held flat in front of you.
          3. At each place (room, toilets, lift, stairs…) tap “Add place here”.
          4. Come back past places you added: the app asks “Back at …?”, and one tap straightens the whole map.

          It maps where you walk, not the walls. Your location stays on your phone until you share the map.
