2Layers → walls & doors

Walls/columns/glazing = Wall (blocks movement). Door swings = Door (passable). A layer holding both = Walls + doors (long straight lines block, arcs and door leaves don't). Furniture you can't walk through (counters, fixed seating) = Wall too.

1. Pick Calibrate, click two ends of a known dimension, enter its real length.
2. Pick Wall and trace walls (click points, double-click/Esc to finish, Shift = straight). Leave gaps at doors.

Scale not calibrated (assumed 50 px = 1 m).
