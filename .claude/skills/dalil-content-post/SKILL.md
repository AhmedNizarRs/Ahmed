# DALIL social post

## Steps

1. **Pick the pillar** (from the `dalil-marketing` agent): problem made visible · how it's made · with the community · founders in Sharjah · accessibility for everyone.

2. **Check the facts** in `dalil-hq/DALIL-facts.md`. Only claims that are true today.

3. **Write**:
   - **LinkedIn** (main channel): a first line that stops the scroll (under 12 words, concrete), 4–8 short lines, one number, one call to action. 3 hashtags maximum, for example `#Accessibility #Sharjah #PeopleOfDetermination`.
   - **Instagram**: caption under 80 words, more visual, same facts.
   - **Arabic version** for each: natural Arabic, not word-for-word. Flag it for a native-speaker check.
   - **Link:** `https://dalil-team.com/?src=linkedin` (or `instagram`, `x`).

4. **Visual brief**: what the image or video shows, using `dalil/DESIGN.md` (stairwell black, bone white, safety yellow). Offer to make it with Canva or image generation. Existing videos and images are in `dalil/site/assets/`.

5. **Alt text** for every image (one or two sentences, describing what matters). Video: note captions are needed.

6. **Check** against `dalil-hq/brand-voice.md`: banned hype words, respectful language, no blindness metaphors. Any person who is blind or has low vision who appears or is quoted needs written consent. Check `dalil-hq/departments/accessibility/feedback-log.csv` or ask.

7. **Add a row** to `dalil-hq/departments/marketing/content-calendar.csv` with status `Draft`.

Never post. The founder publishes.
