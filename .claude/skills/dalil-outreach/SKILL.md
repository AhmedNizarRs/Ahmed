# DALIL outreach

## Steps

1. **Who and why.** Confirm the target (person, role, organisation), the audience type (building buyer, community partner, investor, programme) and the one ask (usually a 20-minute call or a site visit).

2. **Research** with web search: the organisation's buildings, recent news, accessibility commitments, the person's role. Find **one specific hook**. Note the source.

3. **Pick the angle** by audience:
   | Audience | Lead with |
   |---|---|
   | Healthcare | Patient independence, accreditation, AED 87k vs AED 118k–263k for wall signs |
   | Education | Inclusion for students and visitors, a campus that guides itself |
   | Towers and real estate | Safety in a blackout (the glow), 1-week install, no renovation |
   | Government and culture | People-of-determination commitments, Arabic braille built in |
   | Community partners | Co-design: "we want blind and low-vision people to shape this" |
   | Investors | AED 170M UAE market, 58% margin, founders building in Sharjah |

4. **Write** in the voice from `dalil-hq/brand-voice.md`:
   - **Email:** subject under 7 words; body under 120 words; hook → problem → DALIL in one line → one proof → one ask.
   - **LinkedIn connection note:** under 300 characters.
   - **WhatsApp:** 2–3 short lines, only for contacts who gave their number.
   - **Follow-ups:** day 3, day 8, day 15, each adding something new (the film, a photo from testing, a relevant fact). Stop after 3.
   - Offer an Arabic version for UAE government, community and local contacts. Write it natively, not translated word for word.
   - Links: `https://dalil-team.com/?src=email` (or `?src=linkedin`, `?src=whatsapp`).

5. **Log it**: add or update the row in `dalil-hq/departments/sales/pipeline.csv` (buildings and partners) or `dalil-hq/departments/fundraising/investor-pipeline.csv` (investors).

6. **Hand over.** Show the draft. With Gmail connected, create a draft only when asked. Never send.
