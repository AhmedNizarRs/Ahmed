# DALIL monthly investor update

## Steps

1. **Gather facts** (only real ones):
   - `dalil-hq/DALIL-facts.md` (roadmap, metrics)
   - `dalil-hq/departments/chief-of-staff/weekly/` (the last 4–5 weekly files)
   - `dalil-hq/departments/sales/pipeline.csv` (count by stage, wins)
   - `dalil-hq/departments/fundraising/investor-pipeline.csv`
   - `dalil-hq/departments/product/certification-tracker.md`
   - `dalil-hq/departments/accessibility/feedback-log.csv`
   - Ask the founder for waitlist numbers (Netlify Forms or the Google Sheet) and site visitors (PostHog), unless given.

2. **Write the update** with this structure, under 350 words:

```
Subject: DALIL — [Month YYYY] update: [one-line headline]

Hi [first name],

[One sentence: the most important thing that happened.]

Highlights
- ...  (3 max, each with a number or a name)

Numbers
- Waitlist sign-ups: X (+Y this month)
- Buildings in pipeline: X (site visits: Y, proposals: Z)
- User-testing sessions: X
- Cash runway: [only if the founder gives it]

Roadmap
- Month 1 Set up: [done / in progress / date]
- Month 6 Certified: [status]
- Month 12 First paying buildings: [status]

What didn't go well
- ...  (1–2, honest, with what we're doing about it)

Asks
- [Specific intro: "an intro to the facilities director at X"]
- [Specific help: "advice on Civil Defence approval for plastics"]

Thank you for building this with us.
Ahmed and Mohammed
DALIL · Make the existing path speak · dalil-team.com/?src=update
```

3. **Check** against `dalil-hq/brand-voice.md`: no hype words, real numbers only, no unconfirmed claims.

4. **Save** to `dalil-hq/departments/fundraising/updates/YYYY-MM.md`. If Gmail is connected and the founder asks, create a **draft** (never send).
