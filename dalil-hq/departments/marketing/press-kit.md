# DALIL press kit

## Boilerplate (English, 60 words)
DALIL is a Sharjah startup that makes the existing path speak. Its 3D-printed clip turns any handrail into braille, raised numbers and a glow in the dark, so people who are blind or have low vision can find floors, exits and rooms by hand. No app, no battery, no renovation. DALIL was founded at the University of Sharjah. dalil-team.com

## Key facts
- 21.8 million people in MENA live with moderate-to-severe vision impairment.
- One clip: braille (Arabic built in), raised numbers, photoluminescent glow.
- Installs in about 1 week, against about 6 for wall tactile signs.
- Designed, printed and fitted in Sharjah.
