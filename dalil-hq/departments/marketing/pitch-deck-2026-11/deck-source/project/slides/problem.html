The problem

Floor numbers, exits and room names live on walls.
Out of reach of a hand.

A person who is blind can walk to the right door and still not know it is the right door. Independence ends at the threshold.

21.8M

people in the Middle East and North Africa live with moderate to severe vision impairment.

Source: DALIL facts sheet

DALIL · The problem

Signs exist, but they are made for eyes. A hand has to search a wall to find them. The handle, the rail and the wall the hand already touches are completely blank.
