Why every building

Every building should
speak to every hand.

You don’t put up an exit sign for the day you expect a fire. You put it up because one day someone will need it.

The visitor you didn’t plan for

A student, a patient, a parent, a guest. You never know who walks in.

Sight changes

People lose sight later in life. The building should not lock them out.

The guidance points here

ISO guidance puts braille at both ends of every stair handrail.

DALIL · Accessible for everyone

Our position: this should be standard in every building, like exit signs. Honest status: UAE codes ask for braille on destination signs and lift jambs, but not yet on handrails. Working with Dubai Municipality, Sharjah authorities and the Emirates Association of the Visually Impaired to bring handrail and door braille into the guides is our biggest growth lever.
