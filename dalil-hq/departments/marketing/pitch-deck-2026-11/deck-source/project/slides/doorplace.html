DALIL · Door and wall

Two touches at every door: the small sticker on the lever, and the wall sticker beside the door at the latch side for longer messages. The 1.4 metre height follows the US ADA range of 1220 to 1525 mm, which we use as a guide until the UAE rule is confirmed.
