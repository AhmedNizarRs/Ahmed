# Target accounts

Order: **our own campus → Sharjah → the UAE.** Sectors: education, government, community, healthcare, transport, culture, real estate, innovation.

Research each one before outreach (`/dalil-outreach`). Move promising ones into `pipeline.csv`. Nothing here is a customer.

## Wave 1: our own campus (now)
- University of Sharjah: start with one building with heavy foot traffic and stairs (library, student centre, a main academic block). Ask for a pilot: one staircase, then the building.
- Neighbouring campuses in University City, Sharjah (other universities and colleges).

## Wave 2: Sharjah (months 3–12)
| Segment | Types of accounts to research |
|---|---|
| Community | Centres and associations serving blind and low-vision people (also partners: coordinate with `dalil-accessibility`) |
| Government | Sharjah government buildings with public counters; municipal service centres |
| Culture | Sharjah museums, libraries, cultural centres |
| Healthcare | Public and private hospitals and clinics in Sharjah |
| Education | Schools, especially inclusive schools and their operators |
| Real estate | Sharjah developers and facility-management companies with residential and office towers |
| Transport | Bus stations and public-transport operators |
| Innovation | Sharjah innovation parks and free zones (they also want show-case projects) |

## Wave 3: UAE (month 12+)
By market size (from `DALIL-facts.md`): towers (3k buildings, AED 62M), education (2k, AED 33M), hotels (1k, AED 33M), healthcare (3k, AED 28M), government and culture (700, AED 9M), malls, airports and metro (150, AED 5M).

## What makes a good first customer
- Visible: the public sees it, and the press might cover it.
- Has a reason now: accreditation, a new accessibility policy, a renovation, a people-of-determination commitment.
- A single decision-maker who can approve a pilot.
- Stairs and handrails that DALIL fits today (round Ø30–50 mm first).
