# User-testing protocol

Use with the `/dalil-user-test` skill.

## Rounds before Month 6
| Round | When | Question | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Month 2 | Is the clip found, and are the ribs understood, without instructions? | v0 prototypes |
| 2 | Month 4 | Braille and numbers: legible at speed, on different rails? | v1 candidate |
| 3 | Month 5 | Full staircase on campus, lights on and off: can people navigate floor to exit? | v1 frozen |

## Each session
- 60–90 minutes per person, 5–8 people per round.
- Two DALIL people: one facilitates, one takes notes. An orientation-and-mobility specialist observes if possible.
- Real stairs (University of Sharjah first), safe and supervised; a spotter on stairs.
- Record measures per task: success (yes / hint / no), time, confidence 1–5, quotes (with consent).

## Safety
- Walk the route beforehand. Remove hazards.
- Never blindfold sighted people as a substitute for blind testers in decision-making tests. (Simulations may be used only for early, internal checks.)
- Lights-off tests: keep a torch and a spotter ready, and stop at once if anyone is uncomfortable.

## After
Log every finding in `feedback-log.csv` the same day. Write the summary in `sessions/`. Send actions to `dalil-product`.
