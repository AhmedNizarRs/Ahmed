# How DALIL works with the community

1. **Nothing about us without us.** Blind and low-vision people shape every version before it ships.
2. **Pay people for their time.** Testers and advisers are compensated. Budget `[TO CONFIRM]`.
3. **Consent is specific.** Separate yes or no for: taking part, audio, photos, video, quotes, using their name. They can withdraw at any time.
4. **Privacy.** Participant codes (P1, P2…) in every file. Names and contacts stay in one private sheet only the founders can open. No medical details unless the person offers them and it matters for the finding.
5. **Accessible by default.** Invitations, consent forms and summaries in Arabic and English, screen-reader friendly, with large print or braille on request. Venues reachable by public transport, with a clear route from the entrance.
6. **Close the loop.** Tell participants what changed because of them.
7. **Respectful language.** Follow `dalil-hq/brand-voice.md`.
8. **Not charity.** DALIL is a product people choose because it works.
