## Findings from public sources (not confirmations)
- 2026-11-08, `founder/competitors.md`: the Dubai Universal Design Code (copy hosted by DHA) asks for braille signage on the door jamb at 1500 mm, and for Grade One braille "that meets the standards in English" on signs. DALIL should carry English braille as well as Arabic. A code consultant confirms.
- ISO (as reported by PBC Today) says tactile information with braille is fitted at the beginning and end of every stair handrail, showing at least the floor number. Check the exact clause (ISO 21542) before quoting it.
- Braille Literacy Canada: braille on handrails only where the floor is flat (handrail extensions of 300 mm or more). Consider placing the clip on the flat end of the rail.
