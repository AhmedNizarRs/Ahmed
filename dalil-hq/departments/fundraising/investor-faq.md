**Why won't buildings just use wall tactile signs?**
Wall signs have to be searched for on a wall. The handrail is already in the hand. DALIL is also cheaper (AED 130 against AED 180–400 per sign), faster to install (1 week against about 6), glows in a blackout and has Arabic braille built in.

**Why not an app with beacons?**
It needs a phone, batteries, hardware maintenance and an app download. DALIL needs nothing. It works when the power fails.
