## Also look for
- Accessibility, inclusion and "people of determination" innovation awards in the UAE and GCC.
- Hardware and assistive-technology challenges (regional and global).
- Corporate innovation programmes of UAE developers, facility-management companies and hospital groups (often a route to a paid pilot).

## What every application needs (keep ready in `data-room-checklist.md`)
One-line pitch, problem, product, market, business model, traction, team, use of funds, roadmap, impact measures, photos and the 15-second film.
