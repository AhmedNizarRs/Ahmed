# DALIL brand voice

Every department writes in this voice: emails, posts, proposals, grant applications, investor updates.

## The voice in one line

Calm, concrete and confident. Like a well-made handrail: it doesn't shout, it just works.

## Rules

1. **Short sentences. Plain words.** "Snaps on in seconds. No tools." Not "Our innovative solution enables rapid deployment."
2. **Concrete before abstract.** Lead with the thing a hand touches: the clip, the rail, "Floor 2. Exit left."
3. **Real numbers only, from `DALIL-facts.md`.** Never round up, never invent. AED, not "dirhams" in tables.
4. **The rhythm of three.** "No app. No battery. No renovation." / "Clip. Find. Read." / "Touch. Sight. Darkness." Use it, don't overuse it.
5. **Respect competitors.** "We welcome competition. Here is why we win." Compare facts, never mock.
6. **No hype words.** Banned: revolutionary, game-changing, disruptive, cutting-edge, seamless, empower, leverage, synergy, world-class.
7. **Arabic and English.** Anything public in the UAE should exist in both. Write the Arabic as Arabic, not a word-for-word translation. Ask a native speaker to check before it goes out.

## Talking about disability

DALIL is built *with* people who are blind or have low vision, not *for* them from a distance.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| people who are blind, people with low vision | the blind, the visually challenged |
| people of determination (the official UAE term, use it in UAE government contexts) | handicapped, disabled people (as a label) |
| independence, finding your own way | helping the helpless, giving them a voice |
| uses a cane, reads braille | suffers from, victim of, confined to |
| accessible for everyone (true: the glow works for everyone in a blackout) | special needs product |

Never use blindness as a metaphor ("blind spot", "turning a blind eye") in DALIL writing.

## Visual identity

See `dalil/DESIGN.md`: stairwell black `#0B0C0E`, bone white `#F2F1EC`, safety yellow `#FFC700` as the only accent, glow green `#8BF5B0` only for the product's own glow. Typeface: Geist and Geist Mono.

## Accessible documents

What DALIL sends out must itself be accessible: real headings, alt text on every image, text contrast of at least 4.5:1, no information carried by colour alone, PDFs tagged for screen readers.
