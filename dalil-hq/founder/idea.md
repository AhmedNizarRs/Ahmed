# DALIL

- What it is: a 3D-printed clip that snaps onto any handrail or handle and makes it readable by touch, sight and in the dark (braille, a raised number, a photoluminescent glow). No app. No battery. No renovation.
- Who it is for: building owners and operators in the UAE who need wayfinding that people who are blind or have low vision can use: universities and schools first (starting with the University of Sharjah), then towers, hotels, hospitals, government and culture buildings, malls, airports and metro. The end users are people who are blind or have low vision, plus everyone in a blackout.
- What it sells, at what price: priced per floor, everything included (site visit, measuring, custom-fit clips and door tags, installation, accessibility map), plus a yearly care plan. AED 130 per clip or door tag. Average building AED 18k; a hospital AED 87k installed. Per-floor rate and care-plan price: [TO CONFIRM].
